# Abyss Theory video production

Channel: Abyss Theory (ocean animals, faceless, animated explainers). Videos are built with Remotion in `video/` and rendered on a RunPod pod with `render.sh`.

## Rules from the channel owner (16 Nov 2026)

- **A visual change at least every 2 seconds (owner, 19 Nov 2026; was 5).** `python3 tools/check_beats.py --video=<name> 2` (run from `video/`) must pass before a render is handed over. Follow `RETENTION.md` for hooks, captions, sound effects and re-hooks; the owner's target is 60–70% average view duration.
- **At least 10 minutes long, shots of 2 seconds or less that match the narration (owner, 22 Nov 2026).** Each shot shows what is being said at that moment, never filler. Split narration into shots of at most 2.0 s for new videos.
- **Every effect needs a reason; audio before effects (owner, 22 Nov 2026).** Hard cuts are the default. A transition appears only when the kind of picture changes or a new kind of moment starts (title, map, stamp, history), never the same type twice in a row, and each has a synced sound (`video/src/kit/edit-rules.tsx`, `planAll`). Light leaks and glitches only at section changes. Danger accents (letterbox, freeze-frame) at most once per section. No camera moves on caption changes; the only added movement is a shake on an impact word (at most one every 10 s). Music ducks under the voice. Effects live in `video/src/kit/fx.tsx` (CSS/SVG, plus WebGL/3D for VFX hero shots).
- **VFX on footage and photos (owner, 25 Nov 2026).** From the next video on, live footage and real photos get visual effects, not just cuts: underwater camera feel (drift, focus pulls, sway), composited particles, light rays, bubbles, caustics and depth haze, a shared colour grade, 2.5D parallax on photos instead of flat zooms, graphics placed on footage, and a few 3D or composite "hero shots" per video (`@remotion/three` with free CC0/CC BY models only). Still one reason per effect. Send the owner a short VFX test clip before building the whole video. No AI-generated images.
- **Shot list, not scenes (owner, 19 Nov 2026).** Every video is a list of roughly two-second shots, each a new visual: simple drawn animation (`video/src/kit/shots.tsx`), free real footage (Mixkit, cut with `tools/cut_footage.py`), or free-licence photos with a drawn fallback. Never use the owner's ai33.pro image-generation credits. Narration is split into shots in `src/<name>/shots.json`; visuals are listed one per shot in `src/<name>/shotlist.tsx`. Shots over 2 s cut in tighter halfway.
- **Retention layer on every video:** word-by-word captions, impact shake and flash, chapter cards, "coming up" teasers and sound effects (`video/src/kit/retention.tsx`, driven by `tools/build_captions.py`). Sound effects are free Mixkit files in `video/public/sfx`.
- **Copy the formula, not the structure.** Study what makes a successful video work (hook type, pacing, payoff) and bend it to an adjacent angle. Never reproduce another channel's titles, thumbnail look, on-screen graphics or scene order one to one.
- **Ukrainian-language videos (owner, 22 Nov 2026):** the humpback video (`Humpback`, `src/humpback/`) tests the Ukrainian market. Script `script/05-humpback-whale-uk.md`; narration by a native Ukrainian voice from the ai33.pro library (`elevenlabs_fs9omVbVxtW4Sh0OZ0ii`, set with `AI33_VOICE`, speed 1.0), not the "Deep sea" clone. After `voiceover.py`, run `tools/align_words.py <script> <name>` so captions use the script's spelling. All on-screen text, instruments and metadata in Ukrainian. The English version (`HumpbackEn`, `src/humpback-en/`, script `05-humpback-whale-en.md`, "Deep sea" voice) reuses the same 497 shots: `tools/translate_shotlist.py` makes its shot list, and `tools/map_shots.py` re-times the shots onto the English narration sentence by sentence (both scripts must have the same sentences).
- Voiceover uses the owner's ai33.pro cloned voice "Deep sea" (`clone_2719063`); the owner has confirmed the rights to it. Generate at 0.95, then speed up 1.1× without pitch change using `tools/tempo.py` (about 178 words a minute). Keep scene lead-in and tail short (`edit.json`: 0.27 s and 0.4 s).

## Channel identity (ours, keep it distinct)

The owner approved this style on 16 Nov 2026 for all videos after the octopus one (the octopus video stays as rendered). Decided after studying two reference videos frame by frame (Depth Theory's sperm whale video and Ice Wild's elephant seal video). The first octopus cut looked too much like Depth Theory (flat diver and animal on dark teal, all-caps captions); do not repeat that look.

- **Rotate formats (owner, 26 Nov 2026; replaces the single encounter framing).** YouTube demonetizes channels whose videos feel interchangeable ("inauthentic content"). Work through `video/research/2026-11-titles.md` in its rotation table: encounter ("What Happens When a ___ ___ You") at most one video in four, the rest story, myth, mystery, depth journey, biology, versus/ranking, prehistoric. No two neighbouring videos share a format; the story arc, opening and thumbnail layout follow the format.
- **Music rotation (owner, 26 Nov 2026):** the first two videos from the title list reuse the music we have; from video three on, rotate ten tracks the owner downloads and sends (give the owner the list when video two is delivered).
- **No corner instruments, no mask, no evidence chips (owner, 25 Nov 2026; replaces 17 Nov).** Do not show the depth, air, dive time or pulse readouts in the corners, or any other text in the frame corners. Do not draw the dive-mask frame. Do not show LOGGED / REPORTED / MYTH labels; sources go in the script and the description instead. A large MYTH stamp is fine when the narration itself calls something a myth.
- **Alternate the four kinds of visual (owner, 25 Nov 2026).** Rotate VFX shots, real photos, real footage and our animation through every section rather than long runs of one kind.
- **Real-people and real-place inserts (owner, 25 Nov 2026).** When the narration names a real person, place, ship or event, overlay a circular or rectangular insert of a real free-licence photo (Wikimedia Commons CC0/PD/CC BY, NOAA, official press images only where the licence allows) on the animation, with a small credit. If no free photo exists, use a drawn portrait or map insert instead, never an invented "photo".
- **Real photos of the animals, always (owner, 26 Nov 2026).** Viewers want to see the real animal: every named animal gets real photos, not only drawings. Search the iNaturalist API first (`api.inaturalist.org/v1/observations?taxon_id=…&photo_license=cc-by,cc0`, works without a key; look up higher taxa with `/v1/taxa?q=`), then Openverse, NOAA and Commons. No watermarks. Use a related species with an on-screen label when the exact one has no free photo. Do not use photos from random sites without a free licence: copyright claims can demonetize the channel (told the owner 26 Nov 2026). When no free photo of an important animal, person or place can be found, do not draw it: tell the owner which images are missing (exact species or subject and the shot it is for) and the owner downloads and sends them (owner, 26 Nov 2026).
- **Animation is the main style (owner, 17 Nov 2026).** Most of every video is flat 2D animation of the animal and the action, in the manner of Depth Theory's animated explainers: characters moving in a scene, cutaways, scale comparisons, diagrams. This is the fallback whenever live footage is not available. Text-on-a-grid cards are not a substitute for animation.
- **Order of use:** animation most; then live footage or photorealistic video clips, used occasionally; then still photos, only a few per video. The first crocodile cut overused stills and text cards; do not repeat that.
- **Footage sources:** free-to-use only. Mixkit works without a key (clip pages list direct MP4 links); Pexels stopped issuing API keys in Nov 2026. Also NOAA and Wikimedia Commons CC0/public domain/CC BY. If a clip shows a related species, say so on screen. Record every clip's source and licence in `video/footage/SOURCES.md`. Never use clips from other channels.
- **Keep it ours:** the animated scenes still use our own characters, colours and drawing style, never Depth Theory's own graphics, titles or scene order.
- **Open with a promise:** a hook in the first 15 seconds, the question by 30 seconds, then a short on-screen list of what the dive will reveal.
- **Sound:** a continuous instrumental music bed under the voice (no human voice in the music); both references have almost no silent moments. For the crocodile video the owner allowed music from ai33.pro (17 Nov 2026); for later videos the owner generates the music and sends it, so do not spend ai33.pro credits on music unless told to.
- Type: Montserrat 800 for headlines, a monospace face for instrument readouts. Colour: deep teal, yellow accent, HUD cyan.

## Workflow

1. Script in `video/script/NN-name.md`, one `## N. Title` section per scene, `**Visual:**` notes, `[CHECK]` on any claim without a primary source.
2. `python3 tools/voiceover.py script/<file> <name>` generates per-scene audio and word timings.
3. Scenes cue visuals to spoken phrases with `makeCue`; `tools/check_cues.py` and `tools/check_beats.py` validate them.
4. Review stills with `tools/stills.mjs`, then the owner renders on the pod with `bash render.sh <Composition>` (`Main` = octopus, `Croc` = crocodile, `Humpback` = humpback in Ukrainian, `HumpbackEn` = humpback in English).
5. Checks take the video name: `python3 tools/check_cues.py --video=croc` and `python3 tools/check_beats.py --video=croc 5`.
