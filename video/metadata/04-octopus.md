## Title

What Happens When a Giant Pacific Octopus Grabs You

## Description

You're twenty metres down when an arm thicker than your wrist reaches out of the rock and wraps around your hand. It isn't attacking. It's tasting you.

The giant Pacific octopus is the largest octopus on Earth: no bones, three hearts, blue blood, more than 2,000 suckers, and a mind spread through eight arms. It eats sharks and seabirds, recognises individual people, and plays. So what actually happens when one decides to hold on to you?
