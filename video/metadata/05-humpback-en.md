What Happens When a Humpback Whale Swallows You

## Description

In February 2025, a humpback whale took a kayaker named Adrián into its mouth for a few seconds, yellow kayak and all, while his father filmed every second. In 2021, lobster diver Michael Packard spent around 30 to 40 seconds inside a humpback's mouth. So what really happens when a humpback whale swallows you? Can it actually swallow you? And why does this same whale save seals from killer whales?

On this dive:
• how a mouth with no teeth takes in tonnes of water
• why a humpback's throat will never let a person through
• the true story of the 1891 "man in the whale", and why it's a myth
• bubble nets, songs that travel across the ocean, and one of the longest migrations on Earth
• what to do so this never happens to you
