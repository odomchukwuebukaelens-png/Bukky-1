## Description
In 1909, a museum in New York built a megalodon jaw so big people could sit inside it. The jaw was wrong. And so was almost everything we pictured behind it.

For more than a century, megalodon has been drawn as a great white shark, only bigger. In this video we follow the evidence: the "tongue stones" that turned out to be shark teeth, why a shark made of cartilage leaves almost nothing but teeth, the family tree that fell apart, the 141 vertebrae of one megalodon in Brussels, the 2022 3D model, and the 2024–2025 studies that point to a longer, slimmer shark of up to about 24 metres. Plus: why it was warm-blooded, what it ate, why it vanished, and why every picture of it, including ours, is still a best guess.
