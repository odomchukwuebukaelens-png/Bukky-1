## Description
You step off a boat above the Mariana Trench and start to sink. Eleven kilometres of water are below you, and something is waiting at every depth.

In this dive we go down zone by zone, from the sunlit surface to the deepest point on Earth. We meet the shark that circled the survivors of the USS Indianapolis, the swordfish that heats its own eyes, the little shark that bit chunks out of navy submarines, the giant squid nobody had photographed alive until 2004 and the whale that hunts it. Further down: a fish that sees with a light no one else can see, the mammal that dives deeper than any other, and the last fish there is. Then comes what the first people at the bottom said they saw, and what a camera found waiting there in 1998.
