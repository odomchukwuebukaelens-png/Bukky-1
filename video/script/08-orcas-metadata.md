## Description
On 4 May 2023, three orcas came up behind a Swiss yacht in the Strait of Gibraltar. The big one rammed the hull. The two small ones went for the rudder… and copied everything it did.

This is the true story of the Iberian orcas: fewer than forty animals, every one of them with a name, and a habit that has sunk boats off Spain, Portugal and Morocco since 2020. We follow it from the first touched rudder to the boats that went down, the revenge theory, what scientists concluded in 2024, and what was found on one of the oldest orcas in the strait in August 2026.
